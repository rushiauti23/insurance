Insurance document processing, from first principles

A policy is a contract. Every document you handle is evidence of that contract, and every field you enter is a claim about it. Learn the few ideas underneath the work and the procedures stop being things to memorize.

Start with the instrument on the right. Type what the source says and what you entered. The page marks the deviation, names the error type and shows the control that prevents it.

Before you use this. Adapt every step to your company's official SOPs, systems and compliance rules. Never put client, policy, PHI or PII data into personal tools. Every example on this page is fictional.

                Ninety days, then a hub you maintain

                First principlePrevention beats detection. Build the habits that raise first-pass yield, then store what you learn where the team can reuse it.

                Daily habits

                • 10 minutes reviewing yesterday's errors.
                • One new glossary term per day.
                • One self-audit per day.
                • End-of-day 5-minute desk reset.

                Weekly

                Every Friday, pick one repeat error and design a prevention. Share it in the team huddle and re-audit after one week.

                Knowledge hub, in your approved wiki

                Build it in SharePoint, Notion or OneNote, whichever your company approves. Tick a section when it exists. Tag everything by line of business, document type, system, error type and difficulty. Keep one owner, a monthly review, version numbers and a last-updated date.

                First principleOne wrong field spoils a whole case. The stricter the measure, the earlier it warns you.
